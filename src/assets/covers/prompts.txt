Solid background color {BG} with a subtle radial light burst behind the character and two or three tiny floating shapes (stars, sparkles, confetti). Centered composition, the character fills about 70% of the image height. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no UI, no watermark, no realistic rendering, no 3D render look. Aspect ratio 16:10.
